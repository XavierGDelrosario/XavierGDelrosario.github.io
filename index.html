01

DINO — Language Learning App

Jun 2026 – Present
Full-Stack Web & iOS App · Language Learning

A Japanese ↔ English vocabulary app that turns any pasted text — subtitles, an article, a transcript — into a color-coded word-by-word reader and a spaced-repetition quiz, then reads the topic to suggest related vocabulary at your level.

  • Runs morphological analysis (kuromoji) in the browser to color-code a pasted sentence word-by-word by how well you know it, resolving inflections to their dictionary form (行った → 行く).
  • Self-hosts a 217k-entry JMdict + WordNet dictionary in Postgres, demoting paid Google Translation to a cache-miss fallback — with atomic quota reservation, always-on global caps, and a kill switch that make overspend structurally impossible.
  • Builds the differentiator on Postgres pgvector: ~41k embedded entries expanded into topic-related vocabulary, ranked by domain centrality and filtered to the user's calibrated level.
  • Runs a spaced-repetition scheduler on a measured forgetting curve in Postgres; three scheduling bugs found and fixed by measuring the model against real recall data.
  • Enforces authorization in the database (RLS, SECURITY DEFINER guards, an append-only review log), proven across 1,600+ tests in 5 layers; live on web and native iOS.
